What this was actually about.
Thread · accountability

The AI issued a confident demand — "obtain MRI" — and carries none of what follows: the cost, the incidental findings, the false alarm, the next scan to chase a shadow. The clinician owns every bit of that. The tool writes the order in spirit; the licensed human signs it in consequence.

Thread · equity

Who gets to be "the prepared patient"? It takes time, literacy, a device, and a sleepless night of caring. Maya's nine pages are a form of privilege. The patient who arrives with nothing isn't less frightened — just less armed. Don't let the printout buy more attention than the quiet patient's symptom.

The framework

This is effort asymmetry walking into your exam room: AI collapsed the cost of producing a workup, so the volume of output soared while its diagnostic value did not. Nine pages, one fear, zero new information. The skill is reading past the volume to the sentence underneath it.
