Module 04 · The unsensed room

The AI read his message. It never saw his face.

AI reasons only on what reaches it. The droop he calls pillow creases, the slur, the fumbled mug, the clock, none of it travels down the wire. You have ten billion sensors in the room. It has the words. The same morning, from all four seats.

What this was actually about.
Thread · equity

The sensing gap compounds the representation gap. A teledermatology model trained mostly on light skin misses melanoma on dark skin; a pulse oximeter reads falsely reassuring on dark skin. When the channel is thin and the training data is skewed, the people already underserved are the ones the machine most confidently mis-reads. The unsensed room is not equally unsensed.

The framework · the velociraptor test

You have roughly ten billion sensory neurons sampling the room, refined by survival. AI has zero. Until it has to wrestle a velociraptor for dinner, it won't have the contextual awareness evolution gave you. Its answer is only ever as good as the slice that reached it; your value is that you are in the room.
