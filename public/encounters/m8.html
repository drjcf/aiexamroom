Thread · accountability (the resolution)

The whole curriculum turns on one line: what you offload and what you keep. Offload the labor, keep the judgment, the synthesis, the sensing, the decision, the accountability, and AI makes a licensed human better at being one. Move that line the wrong way and every failure in the prior seven modules follows. The good co-pilot is defined entirely by where the line sits.

The framework · real partnership has a tell

A genuine co-pilot is one the human can still overrule, still has to verify, still has to sign for. That isn't friction, it's the point. A co-pilot you can't override isn't a co-pilot, it's an autopilot with your name on the paperwork. Human-in-the-loop is only real when the human can say no and bears the consequence either way.
